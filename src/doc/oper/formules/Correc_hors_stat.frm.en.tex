%%Correc_hors_stat
  Average and standard-deviations of X samples and 
their derivative are computed on a window
of width $fenetre$, moving by steps of $fenetre/10$.

  Every sample matching one of the following criteria are then
replaced by missing\_value.
\begin{itemize}
    \item not in the [$Borne_{inf}$ , $Borne_{sup}$] interval.
    \item or which deviation from the average 
is larger than $Toler\_X . \mathrm{ecart-type}$ 
    \item or which  deviation of the derivative from its average 
is  larger than $Toler\_{dX} . \mathrm{ecart-type}$ 
\end{itemize}
  
  Eventually, consecutives series of missing values
of length less than $seuil\_duree$ are replaced 
by linear interpolation.
