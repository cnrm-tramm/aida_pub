\[
Z = a.X + b
\]
where : \newline
$a$ et $b$ are computed to minimize the mean quadratic
difference between $Z$ and $Y$ :
\begin{align*}
a &= \frac{\overline { (X - \overline{X}) . (Y - \overline{Y})} }
        {\overline {(X -\overline{X})^{2}} } \\
b &= \overline{Y} - a. \overline{X} \\
\end{align*}
