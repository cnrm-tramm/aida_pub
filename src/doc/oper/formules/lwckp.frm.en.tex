% calcul du rapport de melange en eau liquide
% a partir de la mesure du King-Probe. 
\[
LWC = \frac{ P_w - A(T_w-T_s).(\rho.V_p)^\chi}
              { l.d.V_p.[ L+c.(T_{wh} - T_s) ] }
\]
where $L = 2.264.10^6 J.kg^{-1}$ (Water latent heat at $100^\circ C$) and
$c = 4218 J.kg^{-1}$ (Specific water heat)

The computation is made only if $V_p > 50.0 m/s$ and $P_w > 3.0 Watt $
