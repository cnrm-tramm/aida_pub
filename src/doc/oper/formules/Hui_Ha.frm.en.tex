\[
e = \frac{H_{a}}{1000} R_v T_{s}
\]
\[
H_{u} = 100 \frac{e}{e_{wi}(T_{s})}
\]
where :\newline
$T_{s}$ air temperature, in Kelvin. \newline
$R_v = 461.51 J kg^{-1} K^{-1}$ (Specific constante of the water vapor).\newline
The function $e_{wi}(T)$ is the saturated vapor tension over ice.
