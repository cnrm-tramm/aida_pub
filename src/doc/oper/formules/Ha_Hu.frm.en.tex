\[
\text{Ha} = 1000  \frac{\frac{\text{Hu}}{100} . e_{w}(T)}{ R_v . T}
\]
where :\newline
$R_v$ = 461.51  J kg$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$ (water vapour specific constant).\newline
